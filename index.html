Tiltstone – Privacy Policy

Last updated October 15, 2026

Overview

Tiltstone is a single-player game in which you roll a boulder by tilting your phone. It has no accounts, no login, no chat and no social features. This policy covers what the game keeps on your device and what the advertising service it uses (Google AdMob) collects on its own behalf.

What stays on your device

Your progress (crystals, stars, unlocked levels and boulders, upgrades, achievements, settings) is saved on your device only. It is never sent to us or to any server we run. Uninstalling the app deletes it, unless your phone's own backup copied it.

The game reads your phone's motion sensor (accelerometer) to steer the boulder. These readings are used on the device in real time only; they are never stored or sent anywhere.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Tiltstone shows optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch for an in-game reward (double crystals after a level, or one more life in the endless trail). The game never interrupts you with ads. Google AdMob may collect an advertising identifier, IP address and device and usage data to show, measure and protect ads, under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive or sell this data.

Where required (EEA, UK, Switzerland) a consent form appears before personalised ads are loaded. You can change your choice any time in the game under Settings → Privacy & ads, or reset/opt out of the advertising ID in your device settings.

Children

Tiltstone is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Tiltstone – Zásady ochrany soukromí

Aktualizováno 15. října 2026

Shrnutí

Tiltstone je hra pro jednoho hráče, ve které nakláněním telefonu valíš balvan. Nemá účty, přihlášení ani chat. Postup hry (krystaly, hvězdy, odemčené levely a kameny, vylepšení, úspěchy, nastavení) se ukládá jen v tvém zařízení a nikam se neodesílá. Pohybový senzor telefonu slouží jen k ovládání v reálném čase, nic se z něj neukládá ani neodesílá.

Hra zobrazuje jen dobrovolné reklamy s odměnou (Google AdMob), které si sám spustíš. AdMob může podle zásad Googlu zpracovávat reklamní ID, IP adresu a údaje o zařízení. Souhlas s personalizovanými reklamami můžeš kdykoli změnit v Nastavení → Soukromí a reklamy. Hra není určena dětem do 13 let. Kontakt: fery1998@seznam.cz.
